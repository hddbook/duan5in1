HUONG DAN DUA WEBSITE LEN NETLIFY

1. Dang nhap https://app.netlify.com/
2. Vao muc Sites, chon Add new site > Deploy manually.
3. Keo file hoa-binh-web.zip vao khu vuc tai len.
4. Doi Netlify cap dia chi dang: ten-site.netlify.app
5. Mo dia chi tren dien thoai de kiem tra.
6. Gui dia chi website de tao ma QR chinh xac.

LUU Y BAO MAT

- Ai co lien ket hoac ma QR deu co the xem tai lieu.
- robots.txt va noindex han che cong cu tim kiem, nhung khong phai mat khau.
- Neu tai lieu noi bo, can dung dich vu co dang nhap hoac goi hosting ho tro bao ve bang mat khau.

TOI UU DA AP DUNG

- 47 trang duoc chuyen thanh WebP 1440 x 810.
- Chi tai trang dang xem va tai truoc hai trang ke ben.
- Ho tro vuot ngang, phim mui ten, toan man hinh va chia se lien ket.
- Anh duoc luu dem dai han tren CDN cua Netlify.
